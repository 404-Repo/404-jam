(01) the premise

Winnable by anyone

We ran this method ourselves for two days and shipped a kart racer that beats a zero shot build on every frame. The interesting part is what decided each round: never the model, always a choice a person made.

The recipe is public and free. The harness is public. The models are the ones you already pay for. So this jam removes the two things that usually decide an AI game jam, budget and access, and leaves the one that should: what you point the thing at.

Our own build lost blind comparisons against a commercial racer eight times out of eight, then six, then five, and the fix each round was a single property a human named. That is the game here. Find the property nobody else thought to name.

(02) how to enter

Five steps

  1. Sign up for Atlas and claim your credits

    One account gives your agent the image, texture, sky, sound and music generation it needs over MCP, with a fixed grant the moment you sign up. Link to come. The 3D is not generated here: that is what the recipe is for.

  2. Clone the recipe and prove the verifier works

    git clone github.com/404-Repo/404-game-recipe, then npm install and npm run selftest. It runs the verifier against assets broken in known ways and asserts each one is caught. A check nobody has seen fail is decoration.

  3. Give your agent the line and a game to build

    The prompt at the top of this page, with your idea in it. Everything after that is yours: how you split the work, what you loop on, who criticises it and how hard.

  4. Make it pass the gate

    Clear the checklist below on a phone viewport and a desktop one. The harness prints a verdict block, and that block is part of your entry.

  5. Submit before the deadline

    Open a pull request on github.com/404-Repo/game-jam with a link to your hosted game, the verdict block and three sentences on what you tried that nobody else would have. Closes 25 September, 23:59 UTC.

(03) the gate

Machine checked
before a human looks

The same harness we gate our own builds with, in the recipe, run by you before you submit.

This is what makes the jam fair: a judge only ever compares games that actually run, on a phone, at a frame rate, with the player moving. We shipped a build that was unstartable on every phone for a fortnight because every check called into the game instead of pressing the button. The gate presses the button.

Run it as often as you like. It writes a filmstrip of your game in motion, which is also the best way to see what your build looks like to a player.

  • Signal window.__READY__ when loaded and startable, within 8 s on a 4G profile.
  • Start from a real button press, a click and a touch tap, not a debug hook.
  • Publish window.__GAME__ every frame: position in metres, frame rate from real elapsed time, draw calls and triangles.
  • Move when the harness holds a real key, and again when it holds a real finger on a phone viewport.
  • Stay inside its own folder: no path that climbs above it, or it dies the moment it is hosted.
  • Keep to the budgets: 900 draw calls, 1.5M triangles in view, 30 fps on a phone.
  • Log no console errors and fetch nothing that 404s.
(04) rules

One hard rule

geometry

Every object in the game is generated as code through the 404 recipe, by your agent or by the open model on a GPU you rent.No downloaded meshes, no asset store, no photogrammetry, no hand modelling. This is the one hard rule and it is what makes entries comparable.

everything else

Images, textures, sky panoramas, sound and music come from Atlas through your jam credits.Use your own art instead if you would rather, and say so. It will not be held against you.

models

Any agent and any model, named in your entry.We used Claude, both Opus and Fable, and the recipe was built with Claude Code. GPT agents and everything else are welcome; the recipe is model neutral.

team size

One to four people. Solo entries and agent fleets both count as one entry.

ships as

One folder that runs from a static host with no build step, playable on a phone and a laptop.Three.js from a CDN import map is fine. Nothing over 10 MB, ready within 8 seconds on 4G.

originality

Original work only: no trademarked characters, names, logos or sound alikes, in the game or the repo.Being inspired by a genre is the point. Shipping someone's property is not.

licence

Your game stays yours. Entering grants 404 the right to show it, stream it and link it.Final wording from legal.

disqualifies

A game that fails the gate, a downloaded mesh, an entry that only runs on your machine, or a late submission.

(05) judging

Blind, in pairs,
in motion

The method we use on our own rounds, pointed at your games.

Frames are captured from every entry while it is being played, then shown to judges two at a time with the names removed and the order shuffled. A judge says which side is better and why in one sentence. Nobody scores a game out of ten, because a number invites a build to optimise the number.

Then the shortlist is played by hand, on a phone and a laptop, because three real bugs in our last build were found by a person playing and none by instrumentation.

(08) questions

Answers

Do I need a GPU?

No. The default path has your own agent write the geometry, which needs no account, no GPU and nothing to pay for. If you would rather use the open model behind the 404 competition leader, it deploys to a rented GPU from a one click template, on your account.

Do I have to use Claude?

No. Any agent that can read files, run a shell and look at images. The recipe is model neutral and we would like to see what a GPT agent or a mixed fleet does with it. Name what you used in your entry.

Can I use my own art?

Yes, for images, textures and sound. The 3D is the one thing that must come through the recipe as code, because that is what makes entries comparable and it is the whole point of the format.

What does the harness actually do?

It serves your folder the way a static host would, loads it, presses your real start button, drives with real keys and real multi touch, photographs the game while it moves, and asserts the player moved and the budgets held. It prints a verdict block and exits non zero when something is wrong.

Is a small game allowed to win?

Yes, and one probably should. Scope is not a criterion. A short thing that is genuinely good to play beats a large thing that is a tour of assets.

How do I get paid?

Prizes are paid in TAO to a Bittensor wallet address you give us when you submit. Payout terms to be confirmed.

Can I enter more than once?

One entry per person or team. Build as many as you like, submit the one you would want played.

Where do I ask for help?

Discord channel and link to come. Office hours are on the calendar above.

(∞) the page

How a game gets here

An entry appears the moment its pull request is merged, which happens when the harness verdict in it checks out against a run of our own. Nothing is curated in or out before judging.

Every card carries the same four things, because those are what a visitor needs to decide what to play next.

play

A link to the hosted game that works on a phone.

gate

Pass or fail, with the draw calls, triangles and frame rate from the verdict block.

built with

The agent and models named by the entrant.

what I found

Three sentences maximum, in their words.Source links are optional. Some entrants will keep the loop to themselves until judging is over.
