01

Why this jam is winnable by anyone

We ran this method ourselves for two days and shipped a kart racer that beats a zero shot build on every frame. The interesting part is what decided each round: never the model, always a choice a person made.

The recipe is public and free. The harness is public. The models are the ones you already pay for. So this jam removes the two things that usually decide an AI game jam, budget and access, and leaves the one that should: what you point the thing at.

Our own build lost blind comparisons against a commercial racer eight times out of eight, then six, then five, and the fix each round was a single property a human named. That is the game here. Find the property nobody else thought to name.

What you are handed on day one

  • The full asset method: one reference image, three independent attempts, a four sided render, a choice made by eye.
  • A verifier that catches an asset that does not parse, has no back, hovers off the ground or has run away in triangles.
  • A play harness that drives your game with real input and photographs it in motion, so a beautiful parked screenshot cannot fool you.
  • A written list of the traps that produce wrong output silently, gathered over six builds.
  • Atlas credits on sign up for every image, texture, sky panorama, sound effect and music track your game needs.

All of it is Apache 2.0 and stays yours. So does your game.

02

How to enter

  1. Sign up for Atlas and claim your jam credits

    One account gives your agent the image, texture, sky, sound effect and music generation it needs over MCP. Sign up at atlas.design/jam, credits land instantly. The 3D is not generated here: that is what the 404 recipe is for.

  2. Clone the recipe and prove the verifier works

    git clone github.com/404-Repo/404-game-recipe, then npm install and npm run selftest. The selftest runs the verifier against assets broken in known ways and asserts each one is caught. A check nobody has seen fail is decoration.

  3. Give your agent the line and a game to build

    The prompt at the top of this page, with your idea in place of <anything>. Everything after that is yours: how you split the work, what you loop on, who criticises it and how hard.

  4. Make it pass the gate

    Your submission has to clear the checklist below on a phone viewport and on a desktop one. The harness prints a verdict block; that block is part of your entry.

  5. Submit before the deadline

    Open a pull request on github.com/404-Repo/game-jam with a link to your hosted game, your repo, the verdict block and three sentences on what you tried that nobody else would have. Closes 12 Oct 23:59 UTC.

03

Rules

Geometry

Every object in the game is generated as code through the 404 recipe, either by your agent or by the open model on a GPU you rent.No downloaded meshes, no asset store, no photogrammetry, no hand modelling. This is the one hard rule and it is what makes the entries comparable.

Everything else

Images, textures, sky panoramas, sound effects, music and concept art come from Atlas through your jam credits.Use your own art instead if you would rather, and say so in the entry. It will not be held against you.

Models

Any agent and any model you like, named in your entry.We used Claude, both Opus and Fable, and the recipe was built and tested with Claude Code. GPT agents and everything else are welcome, and the recipe is written to be model neutral.

Team size

One to four people. Solo entries and agent fleets both count as one entry.

Ships as

One folder that runs from a static host with no build step, playable in a browser on a phone and a laptop.Three.js from a CDN import map is fine. Nothing over 10 MB, ready within 8 seconds on a 4G profile.

Originality

Original work only: no trademarked characters, track names, logos or sound alikes, in the game or in the repo.Being inspired by a genre is the point. Shipping someone's property is not.

Licence

Your game stays yours. Entering grants 404 the right to show it, stream it and link it.Final wording to come from legal.

Disqualifies

A game that does not pass the gate, a downloaded mesh, an entry that only runs on the entrant's machine, or a submission after the deadline.

04

The gate

Every entry is machine checked before a human looks at it. The same harness we gate our own builds with, in the recipe, run by you before you submit.

This is not bureaucracy. It is the thing that makes the jam fair: a judge only ever compares games that actually run, on a phone, at a frame rate, with the player moving. We have shipped a build that was unstartable on every phone for a fortnight because every check called into the game instead of pressing the button. The gate presses the button.

Run it yourself as often as you like. It writes a filmstrip of your game in motion, which is also the best way to see what your build actually looks like to a player.

Your game must

  • Signal window.__READY__ when it is loaded and startable, within 8 s on a 4G profile.
  • Start from a real button press, a click and a touch tap, not a debug hook.
  • Publish window.__GAME__ every frame: position in metres, frame rate from real elapsed time, and the renderer's draw calls and triangles.
  • Move when the harness holds a real key, and again when it holds a real finger on a phone viewport.
  • Stay inside its own folder: no path that climbs above it, or it dies the moment it is hosted.
  • Keep to the budgets: 900 draw calls, 1.5M triangles in view, 30 fps or better on a phone.
  • Log no console errors and fetch nothing that 404s.

Paste the verdict block into your entry. It is the first thing a judge reads.

05

Judging

Blind, in pairs, in motion. The method we use on our own rounds, pointed at your games.

Frames are captured from every entry while it is being played, then shown to judges two at a time with the names removed and the order shuffled. A judge says which side is better and why in one sentence. Nobody scores a game out of ten, because a number invites a build to optimise the number.

Then the shortlist is played, by hand, on a phone and on a laptop, because three real bugs in our last build were found by a person playing and none of them by instrumentation.

Judges: to be announced.

40 percent

Is it good to playFeel, pace, readability at speed, whether the first thirty seconds teach you the game.

30 percent

Does it look like a made thingA coherent set, a light rig with two colour temperatures, a world that reads as one place rather than a scatter of props.

20 percent

What did you findThe clever solution, the mechanic nobody else tried, the trick the rest of us will steal. Say it plainly in your entry.

10 percent

How it was madeThe loop you built, what you let fail your work, what you measured. Show the receipts.

08

Questions

Do I need a GPU?

No. The default path has your own agent write the geometry, which needs no account, no GPU and nothing to pay for. If you would rather use the open model behind the 404 competition leader, it deploys to a rented GPU from a one click template, on your account.

Do I have to use Claude?

No. Any agent that can read files, run a shell and look at images. The recipe is model neutral and we would like to see what a GPT agent or a mixed fleet does with it. Name what you used in your entry.

Can I use my own art?

Yes, for images, textures and sound. The 3D is the one thing that must come through the recipe as code, because that is what makes entries comparable and it is the whole point of the format.

What does the harness actually do?

It serves your folder the way a static host would, loads it, presses your real start button, drives with real keys and real multi touch, photographs the game while it moves, and asserts the player moved and the budgets held. It prints a verdict block and exits non zero when something is wrong.

Is a small game allowed to win?

Yes, and one probably should. Scope is not a criterion. A short thing that is genuinely good to play beats a large thing that is a tour of assets.

Can I enter more than once?

One entry per person or team. Build as many as you like, submit the one you would want played.

Where do I ask for help?

Discord channel and link to come. Office hours are on the calendar above.
