# Tripothon S1 vs 404 game jam, 9 Sep 2026

Source: https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/events/tripothon-s1 rendered in a browser 9 Sep 2026 (page is a JS app; curl returns nothing).

What Tripothon is: Tripo's first "world-building hackathon". Online submissions 15 Sep to 5 Oct, offline demo days in seven cities 5 to 20 Oct, jury review to 25 Oct, winners 25 Oct. Claimed 3000+ participants, 30+ countries, $50,000 pool. Five direction tracks (Game, Film/VFX, VR/XR/AR, App, Physical design) plus partner tool tracks (Tripo, PICO, Heygears, World Labs). Teams of 1 to 3. A separate social media prize (post your process, tag @TripoAI, #Tripothon, counted 20 Oct). Roughly 40 named jury members with photo, title, tags and profile link, mostly academics and creators, one Rockstar artist, one World Labs. Rotating hero line: "Build a world as a Gift / For the moon's dark side / For everyone who never fits in". Two hero CTAs: Start Building (live) and Submit Project (greyed, "submissions open Sep 15"). Countdown in AoE (UTC-12). "Join the Community" link. Dark page, yellow accent, 16,000px tall, 161 images, 4 videos. Own placeholders: "More coming soon" tool tracks, "MORE TO COME" jury.

Overlap with us: their window (15 Sep to 5 Oct) contains our whole jam (11 to 25 Sep). We announce a winner on 28 Sep, they announce 25 Oct.

## Proposals, ranked (for Ben to choose; nothing changed)

1. Say entrants keep their game and may enter it elsewhere, and say Tripothon by name in the FAQ. Our close is 25 Sep, theirs 5 Oct: a 404 jam game can be polished and submitted to Tripothon afterwards. Turns their bigger pool into a reason to enter ours first. Cheap, one FAQ line. Recommend.
2. Named jury wall. Their strongest trust signal is 40 faces with titles. We need five to eight, with photo, title, one tag, link. Include one working studio artist, one indie dev, one Bittensor figure, one Atlas person declared as conflicted. Blocked on you. Recommend.
3. Hero CTAs: keep "How to enter" live and add a greyed "Submit" with "opens 11 Sep" under it. Sets expectation and shows the flow. Recommend.
4. A theme, optional. Their "build a world as a gift, for X" gives an angle. Ours is a mechanic (one line) with no angle. Option: announce a theme at open (classic jam) that slots into the line, e.g. "build me a game about a place that no longer exists", with "any genre" kept. Your call; creativity is 20 percent of the rubric so a theme helps judges compare.
5. Social prize. "Post your build process, tag @404gen_, #404jam"; small prize (an Atlas licence, or 0.25 TAO carved from the community pot). Feeds the build-in-public goal for @404gen_ directly. Recommend if you can fund it.
6. Community link. They have "Join the Community". We have GitHub issues and office hours. A Discord or X Community link in the header and footer is a standard signal and costs nothing but moderation. Your call.
7. Partner or tool track. They reward use of partner tools. Our analogue: an "open model" track for entries whose assets ran on the 404 open weights on a rented GPU, small extra prize. Rewards the thing only we have. Your call; needs the weights question (HF 401) settled first.
8. Countdown timezone: ours should say UTC next to the countdown like theirs says AoE. Trivial. Recommend.
9. Do not copy: the scale claims (we cannot make them), the dark maximal page (ours loads in a fraction), five tracks (our one hard rule is the point), the 40-person jury (five real ones beat forty names).
10. Positioning line to keep in mind for the announcement: they are 40 days, seven cities, $50k, any tool. We are two weeks, one line, no GPU, a phone-playable gate, and every rule final at open. Different jam, and ours finishes before theirs starts judging.
